# Peptide-HLA complex stability — Serova Protein Engineering Track

London AI × Science Hackathon, 3–4 October 2026.

**The question (from the track brief):** can existing protein foundation models improve prediction of
peptide-HLA complex stability over a domain-specific supervised model?

This is deliberately an open research question rather than a leaderboard. The brief states that
NetMHCstabpan is "unfair to use as a direct comparator" because it was trained on the entirety of
this dataset, and that "negative results are just as good as positive results where well supported".
We are therefore optimising for a watertight evaluation, not a score.

## Dataset
28,166 measured peptide-HLA half-lives: 5,633 distinct 9-mer peptides across 75 HLA class I alleles
(36 HLA-A, 39 HLA-B). Columns are `allele`, `peptide`, `thalf_hours` (the target, in hours),
`hla_seq` (the 182-residue α1/α2 groove domain) and `hla_pseudoseq` (34 peptide-contacting positions).

## Data citation

The dataset was generated by Rasmussen et al. and made publicly available. Per the track brief, any
work using it should cite:

> Rasmussen M, Fenoy E, Harndahl M, Kristensen AB, Nielsen IK, Nielsen M, Buus S.
> **Pan-Specific Prediction of Peptide–MHC Class I Complex Stability, a Correlate of T Cell
> Immunogenicity.** *The Journal of Immunology.* 2016;197(4):1517–1524.
> doi:[10.4049/jimmunol.1600582](https://doi.org/10.4049/jimmunol.1600582)

The pseudosequence convention follows the NetMHCpan family:

> Reynisson B, Alvarez B, Paul S, Peters B, Nielsen M. **NetMHCpan-4.1 and NetMHCIIpan-4.0.**
> *Nucleic Acids Research.* 2020;48(W1):W449–W454. doi:10.1093/nar/gkaa379

Allele nomenclature follows IPD-IMGT/HLA (Barker et al., *NAR* 2023;51(D1):D1053–D1060).
